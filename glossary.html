Every symbol and key term from Part I, with a link to where it is explained.

Notation

Follows the book's conventions: scalars lowercase, vectors bold lowercase, matrices uppercase; vectors are columns; gradients are rows.

Glossary A–Z

Affine subspace §2.8
A subspace shifted by a support point: $\mathbf x_0+U$. Solution sets of $A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$ are affine.
Angle §3.4
$\cos\omega=\langle\mathbf x,\mathbf y\rangle/(\|\mathbf x\|\|\mathbf y\|)$.
Augmented matrix §2.3
The matrix $[A\,|\,\mathbf b]$ used to solve $A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$ by row operations.
Automatic differentiation §5.6
Exact derivatives computed by applying the chain rule to elementary steps of a program.
Backpropagation §5.6
Reverse-mode automatic differentiation: forward pass for values, backward pass for gradients.
Basis §2.6
A minimal generating set of a vector space = a maximal independent set. Gives unique coordinates.
Bayes' theorem §6.3
$p(x\mid y)=p(y\mid x)p(x)/p(y)$: posterior $\propto$ likelihood $\times$ prior.
Bernoulli / Binomial / Beta §6.6
Yes-no outcome / number of successes in $N$ trials / distribution over a probability $\mu\in[0,1]$.
Cauchy–Schwarz inequality §3.3
$|\langle\mathbf x,\mathbf y\rangle|\le\|\mathbf x\|\|\mathbf y\|$.
CDF, pdf, pmf §6.2
Cumulative distribution function; probability density (continuous); probability mass function (discrete).
Chain rule §5.1
$(g\circ f)'=g'(f)f'$; for vectors, multiply Jacobians.
Change of variables §6.7
$p_Y(y)=p_X(U^{-1}(y))\,|dU^{-1}/dy|$ (Jacobian determinant in higher dimensions).
Characteristic polynomial §4.1
$p_A(\lambda)=\det(A-\lambda I)$; its roots are the eigenvalues.
Cholesky decomposition §4.3
$A=LL^\top$ for symmetric positive definite $A$, $L$ lower-triangular.
Column space (image) §2.7
Span of the columns of $A$; has dimension $\operatorname{rk}(A)$.
Condition number §7.1
$\kappa=\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}$; large $\kappa$ means a thin valley and slow gradient descent.
Conditional independence §6.4
$p(x,y\mid z)=p(x\mid z)p(y\mid z)$.
Conjugate prior §6.6
A prior whose posterior is in the same family (Beta with Bernoulli).
Convex conjugate (Legendre–Fenchel) §7.3
$f^*(\mathbf s)=\sup_{\mathbf x}(\langle\mathbf s,\mathbf x\rangle-f(\mathbf x))$.
Convex function / set §7.3
Chord above the graph / segment stays inside. Every local minimum of a convex problem is global.
Coordinates §2.7
The unique coefficients of a vector with respect to an ordered basis.
Correlation §6.4
Covariance divided by the standard deviations; in $[-1,1]$; the cosine of the angle between random variables.
Covariance (matrix) §6.4
$\operatorname{Cov}[x,y]=\mathbb{E}[xy]-\mathbb{E}[x]\mathbb{E}[y]$; the covariance matrix is symmetric positive semi-definite.
Defective matrix §4.2
Fewer than $n$ independent eigenvectors; not diagonalisable.
Derivative §5.1
$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h$: the slope of the tangent.
Determinant §4.1
Signed volume scaling of a square matrix; $\ne0\iff$ invertible.
Diagonalisable §4.4
$A=PDP^{-1}$ with diagonal $D$; needs $n$ independent eigenvectors.
Difference quotient §5.1
Slope of the secant line, $\frac{f(x+\delta x)-f(x)}{\delta x}$.
Dimension §2.6
The number of vectors in any basis of a vector space.
Distance (metric) §3.3
$d(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)=\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\|$; positive definite, symmetric, triangle inequality.
Dot product §3.2
$\mathbf x^\top\mathbf y=\sum x_iy_i$: the standard inner product.
Dual problem / duality §7.2
$\max_{\boldsymbol\lambda\ge0}\min_{\mathbf x}\mathfrak L$; weak duality: dual $\le$ primal; strong duality for convex problems.
Eckart–Young theorem §4.6
The truncated SVD is the best rank-$k$ approximation; error $\sigma_{k+1}$ (spectral norm).
Eigenvalue / eigenvector / eigenspace §4.2
$A\mathbf x=\lambda\mathbf x$; the eigenspace $E_\lambda=\ker(A-\lambda I)$.
Elementary row operations §2.3
Swap two rows, scale a row by a nonzero number, add a multiple of one row to another. They keep the solution set unchanged.
Evidence (marginal likelihood) §6.3
$p(y)=\int p(y\mid x)p(x)dx$; normalises the posterior.
Expected value §6.4
$\mathbb{E}[g(X)]=\int g(x)p(x)dx$ (or sum); a linear operator.
Exponential family §6.6
$p(\mathbf x\mid\boldsymbol\theta)=h(\mathbf x)\exp(\langle\boldsymbol\theta,\boldsymbol\phi(\mathbf x)\rangle-A(\boldsymbol\theta))$.
Free / basic variables §2.3
In row-echelon form, variables of pivot columns are basic, the others free (they parametrise the solutions).
Gaussian (normal) distribution §6.5
Defined by mean and covariance; closed under marginals, conditionals, sums, linear maps.
Gaussian elimination §2.3
Row-reducing the augmented matrix to (reduced) row-echelon form to solve systems, invert matrices, find ranks and bases.
General / particular solution §2.3
One solution of $A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$ plus all solutions of $A\mathbf x=\mathbf 0$.
Geometric vs algebraic multiplicity §4.2
Dimension of $E_\lambda$ vs how often $\lambda$ is a root; geometric $\le$ algebraic.
Gradient §5.2
The row vector of partial derivatives; direction of steepest ascent.
Gradient descent §7.1
$\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x-\gamma\nabla f^\top$; finds a local minimum.
Gram–Schmidt process §3.8
Turns a basis into an orthogonal/orthonormal one by subtracting projections.
Group §2.4
A set with an operation that is closed, associative, has a neutral element and inverses. Abelian if commutative.
Hessian §5.7
Matrix of second derivatives; symmetric; measures curvature.
Hyperplane §3.6
An affine subspace of dimension $n-1$; described by a normal vector.
i.i.d. §6.4
Independent and identically distributed.
Identity matrix §2.2
$I_n$: ones on the diagonal; $I A=AI=A$.
Independence §6.4
$p(x,y)=p(x)p(y)$; implies zero covariance, not conversely.
Inner product §3.2
Symmetric, positive definite bilinear map; in coordinates $\mathbf x^\top A\mathbf y$ with $A$ symmetric positive definite.
Inverse matrix §2.2
$A^{-1}$ with $AA^{-1}=A^{-1}A=I$. Exists only for square, regular (non-singular) matrices.
Jacobian §5.3
$m\times n$ matrix of partial derivatives of $\mathbf f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m$; the local linear map.
Joint, marginal, conditional §6.3
$p(x,y)$; $p(x)=\sum_yp(x,y)$; $p(y\mid x)=p(x,y)/p(x)$.
Kernel (null space) §2.7
$\{\mathbf x:\Phi(\mathbf x)=\mathbf 0\}$; for a matrix, the solutions of $A\mathbf x=\mathbf 0$. Dimension $n-\operatorname{rk}A$.
Lagrangian / Lagrange multiplier §7.2
$\mathfrak L=f+\boldsymbol\lambda^\top\mathbf g$; multipliers $\lambda_i\ge0$ for inequalities.
Laplace expansion §4.1
Determinant by expanding along a row or column into smaller determinants.
Law of total variance §6.5
$\mathbb{V}[x]=\mathbb{E}_y[\mathbb{V}[x\mid y]]+\mathbb{V}_y[\mathbb{E}[x\mid y]]$.
Likelihood §6.3
$p(y\mid x)$ as a function of the parameters $x$; not a distribution over $x$.
Linear combination §2.5
A sum $\lambda_1\mathbf x_1+\dots+\lambda_k\mathbf x_k$.
Linear independence §2.5
No vector is a combination of the others; only the trivial combination gives $\mathbf 0$.
Linear mapping §2.7
A map preserving addition and scaling. Every matrix is one, and vice versa given bases.
Linear program (LP) §7.3
$\min\mathbf c^\top\mathbf x$ s.t. $A\mathbf x\le\mathbf b$; optimum at a vertex.
Linearization §5.8
$f(\mathbf x)\approx f(\mathbf x_0)+\nabla f(\mathbf x_0)(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0)$.
Low-rank approximation §4.6
$\hat A(k)=\sum_{i\le k}\sigma_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top$.
Mean, median, mode §6.4
Average; value where the CDF is $0.5$; peak of the density.
Minus-1 trick §2.3
Reading a basis of the kernel off a reduced row-echelon form by inserting $-1$ pivots.
Momentum §7.1
Adds $\alpha\Delta\mathbf x_i$ to the gradient step: a moving average of past gradients.
Norm §3.1
A length function with absolute homogeneity, triangle inequality and positive definiteness ($\ell_1,\ell_2,\dots$).
Normal equation §3.8
$B^\top B\boldsymbol\lambda=B^\top\mathbf x$: finds the projection coordinates onto $\operatorname{span}(B)$.
Orthogonal / orthonormal §3.4
Inner product zero / orthogonal with unit length.
Orthogonal complement §3.6
$U^\perp$: all vectors orthogonal to every vector of $U$; $\dim U+\dim U^\perp=\dim V$.
Orthogonal matrix §3.4
$A^\top A=I$, $A^{-1}=A^\top$; preserves lengths and angles.
Orthonormal basis (ONB) §3.5
Orthonormal basis: coordinates are $\lambda_i=\mathbf b_i^\top\mathbf x$.
Partial derivative §5.2
Derivative with respect to one variable, others held fixed.
Pivot §2.3
The first nonzero entry of a nonzero row in row-echelon form.
Positive definite matrix §3.2
Symmetric $A$ with $\mathbf x^\top A\mathbf x\gt0$ for $\mathbf x\ne\mathbf 0$ (all eigenvalues $\gt0$).
Posterior / prior §6.3
Belief after / before seeing data.
Probability integral transform §6.7
$F_X(X)\sim\mathrm{Uniform}(0,1)$; basis of inverse-transform sampling.
Product rule / sum rule §6.3
$p(x,y)=p(y\mid x)p(x)$; $p(x)=\sum_yp(x,y)$.
Projection §3.8
A linear map with $\pi^2=\pi$; orthogonal projection gives the closest point in a subspace.
Projection matrix §3.8
$P=bb^\top/\|b\|^2$ (line) or $B(B^\top B)^{-1}B^\top$ (subspace); symmetric, $P^2=P$.
Pseudo-inverse §2.3
$(A^\top A)^{-1}A^\top$ for $A$ with independent columns; gives the least-squares solution.
Quadratic program (QP) §7.3
$\min\tfrac12\mathbf x^\top Q\mathbf x+\mathbf c^\top\mathbf x$ s.t. $A\mathbf x\le\mathbf b$.
Random variable §6.1
A function from outcomes $\Omega$ to a target space $\mathcal T$ (neither random nor a variable).
Rank §2.6
Number of independent columns (= rows). Invertible $\iff$ full rank.
Rank–nullity theorem §2.7
$\dim\ker\Phi+\dim\operatorname{Im}\Phi=\dim V$.
Reconstruction (projection) error §3.8
$\|\mathbf x-\pi_U(\mathbf x)\|$.
Regular / singular matrix §2.2
Regular = invertible; singular = not invertible ($\det=0$).
Rotation §3.9
Orthogonal map of the plane by angle $\theta$; $R(\theta)$. Givens rotations generalise to $n$ dimensions.
Row-echelon form (REF / RREF) §2.3
Staircase of pivots; reduced form has pivots equal to 1 with zeros above and below.
Similar matrices §2.7
$\tilde A=S^{-1}AS$: the same linear map in two bases.
Singular value decomposition (SVD) §4.5
$A=U\Sigma V^\top$ for any matrix; singular values $\sigma_i=\sqrt{\lambda_i(A^\top A)}$.
Span §2.6
All linear combinations of a set of vectors; the smallest subspace containing them.
Spectral norm §4.6
$\|A\|_2=\max\|A\mathbf x\|/\|\mathbf x\|=\sigma_1$.
Spectral theorem §4.2
Symmetric matrices have real eigenvalues and an orthonormal eigenbasis.
Step size (learning rate) §7.1
The factor $\gamma$ multiplying the gradient.
Stochastic gradient descent (SGD) §7.1
Gradient descent with a random mini-batch gradient estimate.
Subspace §2.4
A subset closed under addition and scaling (contains $\mathbf 0$).
Sufficient statistic §6.6
A function of the data containing all the information about the parameter (Fisher–Neyman).
Symmetric matrix §2.2
$A=A^\top$.
Taylor series / polynomial §5.1
A function as a polynomial built from its derivatives at $x_0$.
Trace §4.1
Sum of the diagonal $=$ sum of the eigenvalues; $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$.
Transformation matrix §2.7
Columns are the images of the basis vectors in coordinates of the target basis.
Transpose §2.2
$(A^\top)_{ij}=A_{ji}$; $(AB)^\top=B^\top A^\top$.
Variance §6.4
$\mathbb{E}[(X-\mu)^2]=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\mathbb{E}[X]^2$.
Vector space §2.4
A set closed under addition and scalar multiplication (with the usual axioms).
