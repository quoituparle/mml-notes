How each chapter is built

  • The story: plain-language explanation of every section of the book, with an interactive visual (sliders, buttons, presets).
  • "Going deeper" boxes (tinted): the precise definitions, theorems, derivations and the book's worked examples, so nothing from the original is missing.
  • "In short" (one-sentence takeaway), "ML link" (where the idea is used), "Check yourself" (quick questions, answers hidden).
  • Exercises with full solutions: the book's own exercises, solved step by step. Every numerical answer was verified by computer.
  • Cheat sheet at the end of each chapter for revision.

Suggested way to study

  1. Read the main text of a section and play with its visual until it feels obvious.
  2. Open the "Going deeper" box for the formal version and the worked examples.
  3. Close the page, try the chapter's exercises on paper, and only then open the solutions.
  4. Revisit the cheat sheet a day later.
