How to use these notes

  • Read a section, then play with its visual. Every chapter has sliders and buttons (the preset buttons are good starting points).
  • "In short" boxes are the one-sentence takeaway. "ML link" boxes say where the idea is used. "Check yourself" questions hide their answers until you click.
  • Each chapter ends with a cheat sheet table for revision.
